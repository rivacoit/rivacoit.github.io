bitcoin-fraud-detection
Transformer-Based Temporal Aggregation for Illicit Bitcoin Transaction
Detection — CS229 Machine Learning, Stanford
- Replaced EvolveGCN's recurrent weight evolution with a causally-masked
  Transformer encoder to capture long-range temporal dependencies in
  dynamic Bitcoin transaction graphs.
- Designed a leakage-free evaluation protocol exposing inflated baselines
  in prior work; reproduced EvolveGCN-O and ran feature-only baselines
  (XGBoost, Logistic Regression) on the Elliptic dataset.

fake-job-detection
Hybrid Fraud Detection for Fake Job Postings — CS152 Trust & Safety, Stanford
- Designed a hybrid rule-based + LLM (Gemini 2.5 Flash) classifier with a
  moderator-feedback loop that auto-injects closed decisions as in-prompt
  few-shot examples; F1 0.913 / 0.95 fraud recall / 4.5x lower cost than
  pure-LLM on a 200-example balanced eval.
- Owned ML and backend integration on a 5-person team; shipped the
  production classifier on a Next.js / Supabase / Vertex AI stack with
  fail-closed routing and a three-approach offline evaluation harness.
